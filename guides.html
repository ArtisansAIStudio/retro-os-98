Game guides

Controls, rules and tips for everything in Basement 98. Open the desktop and double-click an icon, or press Start > Programs > Games.

Quick tips
  • Wins earn sweets. Open My Sweets to see your collection.
  • Games with a score end with a box asking for an arcade tag and country for the leaderboard. Use a nickname, not your real name.
  • On a phone, windows open full screen. Blockcraft Classic needs a computer.

Play on its own page

Slam Book: make your own, share the link, and read your friends' anonymous notes. Only you can read them.

FLAMES: the classic notebook name game, on its own page.

Dirt Dash: a retro motocross race. The bike drives itself; use Up/Down for lanes, Space or X for turbo, Left/Right to tilt in the air.

Games

FLAMES

Type your name and a partner's name. The game crosses out letters that appear in both names, counts how many letters are left, then uses that number to eliminate the letters F, L, A, M, E, S in a circle until one remains. The survivor decides the result: Friends, Love, Affection, Marriage, Enemies or Siblings. It is pure fun with no science behind it. Try your friends, your teachers and your favourite cricketers.

Book Cricket

Turn a page to bat. The last digit of the page number decides what happens: 0 is out, 1 to 6 are runs and 7, 8 and 9 are dot balls. You get 12 balls and one wicket. Then the computer chases your score. Your best first innings goes on the leaderboard.

Snake

Use the arrow keys or WASD, swipe on a touch screen, or tap the on-screen arrows. Eat food to grow. Hitting a wall or yourself ends the run. Tip: plan your turns early and keep the snake along the edges so the middle stays open.

Tic-Tac-Toe (XO)

Play a friend or the computer. The computer plays well but is not perfect, so a draw is a respectable result. Tip: start in the middle or a corner.

Name Place Animal Thing

You get a random letter and 60 seconds to fill in a name, a place, an animal and a thing that all start with it. Every valid answer is worth 10 points. Press STOP when you are done, as in the playground. The game only checks the first letter, so argue the rest with your friends.

Hangman

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before the drawing is finished. All the words are things from the 90s, like cassette, pager and tuck shop. Tip: try vowels first, then common consonants like R, S and T.

Stone Paper Scissors

The classic. Pick one and the computer picks at the same time. Stone beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats stone. Every third win earns a sweet.

Dots and Boxes

Take turns drawing a line between two dots. Whoever draws the fourth side of a box claims it and goes again. The computer always takes a box when it can and avoids giving you easy ones. Tip: avoid drawing the third side of any box.

Minesweeper

Click a square to reveal it. Numbers tell you how many mines touch that square. Flag suspected mines with a right-click, Shift-click or a long press on a phone. Click a number whose flags are all placed to open the rest of its neighbours. There are three sizes, and times go on the leaderboard. Tip: a 1-2-1 pattern along a wall usually means mines under both 1s.

Solitaire

Klondike with one card drawn at a time. Click a card, then click where it should go. Double-click a card to send it to the foundations. Build the tableau down in alternating colours and the foundations up by suit from Ace to King.

Turbo Rush (split-screen racer)

Race three laps. Player 1 uses W, A, S, D. Player 2 uses the arrow keys. You can also race the computer on its own. The best time against the computer goes on the leaderboard. Tip: let off the gas into tight corners instead of sliding onto the grass.

Maze Hunter 3D

A first-person maze with five guards. Move with W and S, turn with A and D, strafe with Q and E, fire with Space or a click. Beating a guard drops ammo, so do not waste shots. Your clear time is ranked.

Blockcraft Classic

A block-building sandbox. Click to capture the mouse, then move with WASD, jump with Space, break blocks with the left button and place them with the right. Keys 1 to 6 choose a block. It needs a computer with a mouse, and it loads a 3D library from the internet.

Block Drop

Falling blocks. Move with the left and right arrows, rotate with Up, drop faster with Down and slam a piece down with Space. Clear lines to score and to speed the game up.

Paddle Wars

Pong for one or two players. Player 1 uses W and S, player 2 uses the arrow keys. First to seven wins. The ball speeds up with every hit.

Jungle Commando

A run-and-gun for one or two players. Player 1 uses A, D, W and F. Player 2 uses the arrow keys and the / key. Jump pits, beat the soldiers and take down the boss at the end.

Brick Breaker

Bounce the ball off your paddle to clear all the bricks. Move with the mouse or the arrow keys and press Space to launch. Where the ball hits the paddle changes its angle.

Alien Invaders

Move with the arrow keys and fire with Space. Clear every alien before they reach the ground. They speed up as you thin them out, and each new wave is faster.

Dot Chomper

Eat every dot in the maze while four ghosts chase you. A big dot makes the ghosts vulnerable for a few seconds. Use the arrow keys or WASD.

Road Hopper

Hop across the road and ride the logs across the river to reach the five pads at the top. Use the arrow keys. Cars and water cost a life.

Rock Blaster

Spin with the left and right arrows, thrust with Up and fire with Space. Big rocks split into smaller ones. The screen wraps around.

Bike Brawl

A motorbike race with fighting. Steer with A and D, gas with W, brake with S, and punch left or right with J and K. Punching a rival who is beside you knocks them off. Crashing into traffic and getting punched drains your stamina, and at zero you are busted. Finish first to win.

Programs and tools

Notepad

Type and save plain text files. They are stored in My Documents on your own device and are never uploaded. Open an existing note from the File menu.

Paint

Draw with the pencil, brush, spray can, shapes and fill tool. Left-click uses the front colour and right-click uses the back colour. Save your drawing as a PNG from the File menu.

Calculator

A simple calculator with memory buttons. You can type with the keyboard too.

DOS Prompt

A pretend command line. Type help to see what works. Try dir, cd, type, ver or the name of a game.

Media Player

Plays three tunes made for this site. You can open your own music files with File > Open, or drag audio files onto the window. Your files stay on your device.

NetSurfer

A web browser from an imaginary 1998. It dials a modem, which does not end well.

Control Panel

Change the wallpaper, use your own picture, pick a colour or switch on the starfield screen saver. You can also drag the desktop icons around and reorder the Start menu items.

Leaderboards and the daily challenge

The Hall of Fame shows the best scores for Snake, Block Drop, Turbo Rush, Maze Hunter 3D, Minesweeper (all three sizes) and Book Cricket. Pick a game and choose World, My country or Today's daily. The Daily Challenge gives everyone on Earth the same Snake food pattern, the same Block Drop piece order and the same Minesweeper board for that day, and resets at midnight UTC.

Scores are verified on a server. See the FAQ for how that works, and privacy for what is stored.

There are secrets

The desktop hides a few surprises. Curious people find them. A hint: look in the folders, and try the keys you would have typed at the school computer lab.
