Basement 98

No windows down here. Just games.

A free, fan-made desktop from the late 1990s that runs in your browser. Boot it up, double-click an icon and play. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

▶ Play Basement 98

What is it?

Basement 98 looks and works like a computer desktop from the late 90s: a boot screen, a Start menu, draggable windows and a taskbar with a clock. Inside are more than twenty original games, a few small programs, a worldwide leaderboard and some surprises we are not going to spoil.

Games you can play

  • Playground classics: FLAMES, Book Cricket, Name Place Animal Thing, Hangman, Stone Paper Scissors, Dots and Boxes and Tic-Tac-Toe.
  • Desktop favourites: Minesweeper and Solitaire.
  • Arcade-style games: a split-screen racer, a first-person maze shooter, a block-building sandbox, falling blocks, Pong, a run-and-gun for two, a brick breaker, an alien shooter, a maze chase, a road-crossing game, an asteroid blaster and a motorbike race-and-fight game.

New to a game? Read the game guides for controls and tips.

Win sweets, beat the world

Every win drops a sweet into your collection. Post your score to the Hall of Fame with a nickname and your country, and take on the daily challenge, the same game for everyone on Earth, reset at midnight UTC. Scores are checked by a server so the boards stay honest.

Original and private

All the code, art and sounds were made for this project. Your progress is saved in your own browser, not on our servers. Read the privacy policy and the FAQ for the details.
