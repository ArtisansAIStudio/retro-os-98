Dirt Dash: free motocross game

Dirt Dash is a retro motocross race that runs right in your browser. Your bike drives itself, so you focus on lanes, ramps and turbo. Beat three rivals to the finish and post your time to the world leaderboard. Free, no download, no sign-up.

Open it inside the full Basement 98 desktop

How to play

  • Up / Down: change lane.
  • Space or X: turbo. It heats up your engine, so use it in bursts.
  • Left / Right: tilt your bike in the air to land on both wheels.
  • On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons.

Tips

  • Take ramps straight and level out before landing. A bad landing slows you down.
  • Save turbo for flat stretches and cool down before the next burst.
  • Switch lanes early to dodge mud and rival bikes.
