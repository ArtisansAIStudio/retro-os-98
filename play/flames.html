FLAMES game online

FLAMES is the name game many of us scribbled on the back page of a notebook. Type your name and another name, and it tells you one of six results: Friends, Love, Affection, Marriage, Enemies or Siblings. It is free, needs no sign-up, and runs in your browser.

How FLAMES works

  1. Write both names and cross out every letter that appears in both. Each letter is crossed out once per match.
  2. Count the letters left over. Call that number N.
  3. Write F, L, A, M, E, S in a circle. Count N places around it, starting at F, and cross out the letter you land on.
  4. Keep counting N from the next letter, skipping crossed-out ones, until one letter is left. That letter is your result.

What the letters mean

  • F Friends
  • L Love
  • A Affection
  • M Marriage
  • E Enemies
  • S Siblings

Is FLAMES accurate?

No. It is a pastime with no science behind it, and a different spelling of a name can change the answer. Treat it as a laugh with friends, not advice. Try classmates, teachers, cricketers or film stars and compare results.
