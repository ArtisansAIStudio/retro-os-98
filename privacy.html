Privacy Policy and Cookies

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version
  • No accounts. No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress (sweets, saved files, wallpaper, settings) stays in your browser. We can't see it.
  • If you post a score, your arcade tag, country and score are public on the leaderboard. Don't use your real name.
  • Ads are optional. They only load if you choose Accept all. Essential only means no ad cookies.
  • We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

Retro OS 98 is a free, fan-made browser game site at artisansmedia.com/nostalgia, run by the owner of artisansmedia.com ("we", "us"). It is a hobby project inspired by the desktops of the late 1990s.

2. What is stored on your device

We don't set our own cookies. The site uses your browser's local storage, which stays on your device, to remember things so the site works the way you left it:

  • your collected sweets and secrets found;
  • files you save in Notepad and folders you create on the desktop;
  • wallpaper, screen saver, icon positions, Start menu order and other settings;
  • high scores and your arcade tag and country (so you don't retype them);
  • your cookie choice (so we don't ask every visit).

None of this is sent to us. You can erase all of it any time with Start > Settings > Privacy & Cookies > Clear my data on this device, or by clearing site data in your browser.

3. The leaderboard

Posting a score is your choice. When you do, we store in our database (hosted by Supabase):

  • the game, your score and the date and time;
  • the arcade tag you typed (up to 12 characters) and the country you picked;
  • a hashed version of your IP address. We use it only to limit abuse (for example, someone submitting hundreds of scores a minute). We never store the raw address next to your score, and the hash is not shown publicly.

The game, score, tag, country and date are public, anyone can see them on the Hall of Fame. Please don't use your real name, phone number or anything personal as your tag. We keep scores until we delete them. You can ask us to remove an entry (see section 9).

The country on a score is whatever you select. We don't look up your location from your IP address.

5. Cookies and advertising

Retro OS 98 is free and may show ads to cover its costs. Ads are provided by third parties such as Google AdSense. Ad providers and their partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see them, and measure results, and may make ads more relevant to you.

  • Accept all: allows ads and the cookies they use.
  • Essential only: we load no advertising and no ad cookies. The site still works fully.

You can change your choice at any time in Start > Settings > Privacy & Cookies. To learn how Google uses data from sites like this, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, or opt out of many ad networks at youronlinechoices.com (EU) and optout.aboutads.info (US).

6. Why we use data (legal bases)

  • Running the site and preventing abuse (storing settings, scores, hashed IPs): our legitimate interest in providing a working, fair game.
  • Advertising cookies: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

7. How long we keep data

On your device: until you clear it. Leaderboard entries (including the hashed IP stored with them): until we remove them, which we do on request. Hosting and security logs are kept by those providers under their own policies.

8. Children

This site is aimed at adults who remember the 90s. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law says so), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has posted personal information in a tag, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Switzerland, California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold almost nothing that identifies you, the main things you can do are:

  • change your cookie and ad choice in Privacy & Cookies;
  • erase everything on your device with Clear my data on this device;
  • ask us to delete a leaderboard entry: contact us with the arcade tag, country, game and approximate time.

We do not sell personal information. If ads are enabled and you accept, ad partners may collect data as described in section 5. Choosing Essential only prevents that.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The database cannot be written to directly from a browser; scores are accepted only through a server check. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't post anything personal.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes, such as new kinds of tracking, will ask for your choice again.

Retro OS 98 is a fan-made tribute and is not affiliated with any company.
