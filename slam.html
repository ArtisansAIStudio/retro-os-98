What is a slam book?

If you grew up in the 90s or 2000s you probably had one: a notebook that went around the class. Everyone wrote a nickname for you, their first impression, one thing you should change, and a message that was a little too honest. This is that notebook, online, free and private.

How it works

  1. Type your name and make your slam book. It takes 10 seconds.
  2. Share the link on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere. Friends fill it in. They can leave their name or stay anonymous.
  3. Open your private link to read every entry as scrapbook cards. Nobody else can see them, not even people who have the share link.

Privacy and being kind

Entries are readable only with your private link. Keep that link to yourself: it is saved in this browser so you can come back. Be kind, and share only with people you trust. We do not ask for an email or any sign-up. See the privacy policy.
